# The result — two engines, two nulls, and seven leads below the noise floor

Closing record, 2026-08-30. Written to stand alone for someone who was not here,
and to be read as evidence rather than as a story. Every number is traceable to a
committed artifact; provenance is stated where a value is a proxy.

---

## 1. The two questions, and the two answers

| | question asked | answer | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 — exits** | can a better exit rescue this lane? | **NO** | zero of 396 exit configurations profitable on SPY over **2,115 trades** |
| **Phase 2 — entries** | is there a positive-expectancy entry region? | **NO** | **104,680 candidates**, **2,620 days**; primary *and* secondary criteria both failed on the pessimistic arm |

**The two results are independent.** They were measured on populations that do not
overlap: Phase 1 on an opening-range-break entry, Phase 2 on a 30-minute grid the
exit lane never saw. Neither conclusion is a restatement of the other.

## 4. Why no rerun at a different stop width was needed

**G1 −0.0676 · G2 −0.0773.** G1 trails, scales out and moves stops. G2 makes *no
decisions at all*. They land **0.01 R apart** while their distributions differ
completely (sd 1.29 vs 4.44; 25.4% winners vs 49.2%).

The units are comparable for a specific reason: **G2's R denominator stays pinned
at 1×ATR while the stop is removed.** A literal `k_stop = ∞` would drive the
denominator to infinity and R to zero; holding it fixed is what makes a
no-stop policy measurable in the same units as a tight-stop one.

So G1 and G2 bracket the geometry range — a stop that binds constantly and no stop
at all — and both are negative and nearly equal. **Interpolating between two
negative points 0.01 R apart cannot produce a sign change.** Stop geometry changes
what an entry earns; it does not create state-dependence that is not there.

This is also Phase 1's conclusion arriving a second time, unprompted, inside a
single Phase 2 table: **the exit policy redistributes; it does not create.**

## 6. The new standing rule — MDE at discovery

**Every candidate effect gets a minimum detectable effect computed at discovery,
against the sample that produced it, before it proceeds to an adversarial pass or
a pre-registration. If the observed effect is below its own study's detection
threshold it is recorded as BELOW NOISE FLOOR and stops there. It does not become
a lead.**

Implemented in `gate/discovery.py` (lane-neutral; reuses `mechanisms.mde`, never a
second formula) and enforced the same way the `N_days` rule is — by a scanner that
fails when an effect is emitted without its MDE, routed through the `Finding`
header rather than by widening a regex.

### Why robustness checks cannot substitute for it

C5_gap survived: monotone gradients on two conditioners, 68% of configs positive
in the top cell, an even spread across eleven years, and a full adversarial pass.
**Every one of those is a robustness signal. None of them is an effect size.** A
below-floor effect can be perfectly consistent — consistency is about agreement,
not magnitude. Only the arithmetic catches it.

### Retro-application — 7 of 7 leads fail

`sd needed` is the per-unit dispersion at which the lead would have cleared;
measured per-candidate sd is **1.293**, so every value below that is a lead that
cannot clear at any plausible dispersion.

**Every positive number this repository has produced is smaller than the detection

The rule independently reproduces every negative verdict already reached by other
means — the SPRT's `ACCEPT_H0`, the max-statistic null, `NO_SUPERSEDE`,
`NOTHING_QUOTABLE` — but reaches them **at discovery**, before the expensive
machinery runs. That agreement is the rule's validation.

Two findings from applying it, both recorded because they sharpen it:

- **Row-counting does not rescue C5_gap.** At 38,070 rows (705 trades × 54
  snapshots) it is still 1.20× below floor. It needs 55,131 — the identical figure
  the sealed-half arithmetic produced independently.
- **One retro row was initially wrong in my own table**: I used n=354 for the
  Gate 3a best cell, but 354 is the count of *valued cells*, not that cell's day
  count. Corrected to 53 days, it is 5.4× below floor rather than 1.8× — further
  below, not nearer.

## 7. What this does NOT establish

**One entry family, two instruments, one construction** — a 30-minute grid, both
directions, ATR-based geometry, on SPY and QQQ. That is not "no edge exists in
markets," nor even "no edge exists in these instruments."

Ten pre-registered event studies still stand and say something narrower and more
useful: **magnitude is conditionable and direction is null.** The magnitude axis
has **never been converted into an entry rule** and remains untested. What is
closed is this family, on this lane, by this construction.
