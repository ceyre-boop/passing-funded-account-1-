# The result — two engines, two nulls, and a lead that was never detectable

Closing record, 2026-08-30. Supersedes no earlier record; each one below still
holds its own numbers. This states what was established and, more usefully, what
was ruled out.

## The claim

**On this lane, entered this way, there is no edge — and the two halves of the
system now say so independently, from opposite ends.**

| | question | answer | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 — exits** | can a better exit rescue the lane? | **no** | 4,512 trades, 396 configs, three instruments |
| **Phase 2 — entries** | is there a positive-expectancy entry region? | **no** | 104,680 candidates, 2,620 days, three graders |

Neither result depends on the other, and they were measured on populations that
do not overlap: the exit work ran on the OR-break entry, the entry work on a
30-minute grid the exit lane never saw.

## Phase 1 — exits redistribute; they cannot create

- **Zero of 396 exit configurations are profitable on SPY** over 2,115 trades.
  Not the best one; none. (`CEILING_10Y_RECORD.md`)
- The 12.5% "3 of 24 days" figure everything rested on was a **24-session
  artifact**; the real number is **31–34%**, replicated across three instruments
  and two disjoint periods on 4,512 entries. The headline statistic was fragile;
  the load-bearing one underneath it (Z, the best fixed policy's return) was not.
- Positive Z on NVDA/QQQ is **selection, not edge**: `best − median` (0.0727)
  exceeds the best config's entire apparent return (+0.0583).
- The first real SPRT — trailing disabled against SF-FROZEN-004 — returned
  **ACCEPT_H0 at step 91, deviation 23.0%**, with both arms negative
  (−172.71 R vs −119.00 R). A real null, not a hollow one.

## Phase 2 — the entry family, closed

Gate 3a graded 104,680 legal candidates × 3 exit policies × 2 fill models =
628,080 rows, table reproducing bit-identically. Adjudicated on the **pessimistic**
arm, locked before the table existed.

- **Primary fails:** SPY mean R −0.37795, QQQ −0.33010. The pair rule returns
  HOLDS — both arms agree, and they agree the sign is negative.
- **Secondary fails, and this is the strong part:** best cell mean R **+0.0956**
  (SPY) and **+0.3557** (QQQ) against a null p95 of **+0.4310** and **+0.5028**.
  **The null's 95th percentile exceeds the observed best cell on both symbols.**
  That is not "no edge found" — it is **less exploitable structure than
  randomness produces**.
- **Four sub-periods, all negative, max share 34%.** Nothing regime-specific.
- **No grader exploitation:** the top 1% by the frozen grader sit at the 90.0%
  and 92.8% percentiles of the other two.

### The geometry confound, closed without a rerun

`k_stop = 1.0` was a declared choice, and a rerun at other values was considered
and **ruled out on the table's own evidence**: G1 is a tight stop that binds
constantly (−0.0676), G2 is hold-to-close with no stop at all (−0.0773). Both
endpoints of the range, measured in the same R units because the denominator is
pinned at 1×ATR. **Interpolating between two negative points 0.01 R apart cannot
produce a sign change.** Stop geometry changes what an entry earns; it does not
create state-dependence that is not there.

### The finding that ties both phases together

G1 (trails, scales, moves stops) and G2 (makes **no decisions at all**) land
within **0.01 R** of each other — −0.0676 vs −0.0773 — while their distributions
differ completely (sd 1.29 vs 4.44; 25.4% positive vs 49.2%). **The exit policy
redistributes; it does not create.** Phase 1's conclusion, arriving a second time
inside a single Phase 2 table, on entries the exit lane never touched.

## C5_gap — the sealed half stays sealed, and why

The one live lead. It survived an adversarial pass on significance grounds
(3 of 13 cells outside the band, exact shuffle tail 3/4000, Bonferroni p≈0.0098,
OUT in both halves against a same-half baseline).

**The sealed-half test was not run, deliberately.** The arithmetic:

| n days in cell | MDE at the measured sd (1.2934 R) | vs the +0.0137 R effect |
|---|---|---|
| **12 — the sealed half's C5_gap cell** | **0.9286 R** | **68× too coarse** |
| 37 — every sealed day | 0.5288 R | 39× |
| **705 — the tune-lane sample that FOUND it** | **0.1212 R** | **9×** |

The sealed half is **37 days** (2026-07-07 → 2026-08-26); one tercile of it is
~12. A null was guaranteed before any data was read, and would then have *read*
as "C5_gap failed on the sealed half" when the truth is that the question cannot
be asked of 12 days. Spending the one-shot unseal on a predetermined answer
would destroy the sealed half permanently for zero information.

Waiting does not help: detecting +0.0137 R at 80% power needs **55,131 days** in
the cell. The sealed half grows ~21 days/month — **656 years**.

**And the row that reframes the lead itself:** at n=705, the sample that produced
C5_gap, the MDE was **0.1212 R against an observed effect of 0.0137 R**. The
effect was **9× below the detection threshold of the study that found it**. It is
not "a lead we could not confirm" — it is a number smaller than its own study's
noise floor.

So the tally is not two nulls plus a live lead. **It is two nulls, and a lead
that was never detectable.**

## What this does NOT establish

One entry family was tested — a 30-minute grid, both directions, ATR geometry, on
SPY and QQQ. That is not "no edge exists in markets", nor even "no edge exists in
these instruments". Ten event studies also stand: **magnitude is conditionable
and direction is null**, and the magnitude axis has never been converted into an
entry rule. What is closed is this family, on this lane, by this construction.

## Why the nulls are worth more than they feel

Every one of them is *decisive* rather than ambiguous, and that is the product of
machinery built before the answers existed:

- pre-registrations committed before any fit (specs 043, 045, 046, 047, 048, 049)
- a two-sided SPRT that was shown to **reject a deliberate degradation**, not only
  to recognise a null
- occupancy counted in **days**, after that unit error twice produced a grid that
  looked three times healthier than it was
- a max-statistic null preserving correlation, because 933 correlated cells
  always contain good news
- every guard fault-injected — disabled, the named test failed, restored
- and an adversarial pass that **reversed two of the architect's own rulings**,
  one of them a code bug discarding 65–75% of the transitions it claimed to count

A system that can produce a decisive "no" in nine days is worth more than one that
produces an encouraging "maybe" in nine months.
